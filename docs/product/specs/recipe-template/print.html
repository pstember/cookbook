Lemon Meringue Pie

A buttery shortcrust, a bright lemon filling and soft swirls of toasted Swiss meringue.

Method: one action, one heat setting, one picture per step

  1. 1

    Make the pastry dough

    1. Put the flour and sugar in a bowl.
    2. Add the cold, cubed butter.
    3. Rub the butter in with your fingertips until the mix looks like fine breadcrumbs.
    4. Add the yolk and the cold water.
    5. Press gently until it comes together as a dough.
    8–10 min
  2. 2

    Roll, line and chill

    1. Roll the dough between two sheets of baking parchment, to about 3 mm thick.
    2. Line one 23 cm loose-bottomed tin, about 2.5 cm deep.
    3. Trim the rim.
    4. Patch any cracks with spare dough.
    5. Prick the base with a fork.
    6. Chill for 30 minutes.
    8–10 min + 30 min chillWaitchill 30 min
  3. 3

    Bake the shell

    1. Put a baking tray in the oven.
    2. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
    3. Line the chilled pastry with baking parchment.
    4. Fill it to the rim with baking beans.
    5. Put the tin on the hot tray.
    6. Bake for 15 minutes.
    7. Lift out the parchment and beans together.
    8. Bake for 5–8 minutes more, until the base is dry and golden.
    20–23 minHeatoven 200°C / 180°C fanWait15 min with beansWait5–8 min uncovered
  4. 4
    Illustration to draw: the lemon base thickening as it bubbles

    Thicken the lemon base

    1. Put the cornflour, sugar, zest, lemon juice and water in a saucepan.
    2. Whisk them together.
    3. Set the pan over medium heat.
    4. Stir until the mix is thick and bubbling.
    5. Simmer for 1 minute, stirring all the time.
    4–6 minHeatmediumWaitbubble 1 min
  5. 5

    Warm the eggs

    1. Whisk the yolks and the whole egg together in a separate bowl.
    2. Add a spoonful of the hot lemon mix to the eggs.
    3. Whisk it in.
    4. Repeat with a few more spoonfuls, so the eggs warm up slowly.
    5. Pour the warmed eggs back into the pan.
    2–3 min
  6. 6

    Cook the filling through

    1. Reduce the heat to medium-low.
    2. Stir until the filling bubbles gently.
    3. Measure the temperature with a thermometer, keeping the probe off the bottom of the pan.
    4. Keep cooking until it reads at least 72°C all the way through.
    3–5 minReducemedium-lowWaituntil 72°C throughout
  7. 7
    Illustration to draw: butter stirred in, filling poured into the shell

    Finish the filling

    1. Take the pan off the heat.
    2. Add the cubed butter.
    3. Stir it in.
    4. Pour the filling into the baked shell.
    2–3 minOff heattake the pan off
  8. 8

    Warm the whites and sugar

    1. Put the egg whites and sugar in a clean heatproof bowl.
    2. Set the bowl over a pan of barely simmering water, leaving a gap between the bowl and the water.
    3. Whisk all the time, for 5–7 minutes.
    4. Stir the mix.
    5. Measure the temperature, keeping the thermometer tip off the bowl.
    6. Stop when the sugar has dissolved and the mix reads at least 72°C.
    5–7 minHeatbarely simmering waterWait5–7 min, to 72°C
  9. 9

    Whip to stiff peaks

    1. Lift the bowl off the pan.
    2. Beat with an electric mixer for 5–8 minutes.
    3. Stop when the meringue is glossy and a peak stands upright.
    5–8 minOff heatlift the bowl offWaitbeat 5–8 min
  10. 10

    Spread the meringue

    1. Spoon the meringue onto the warm filling.
    2. Spread it right to the pastry edge.
    3. Lift the spoon as you go to pull up wavy peaks.
    3–4 min
  11. 11

    Toast the peaks

    Option A · oven grill
    1. Preheat the grill until hot.
    2. Put the pie about 15 cm below the element.
    3. Grill for 1–2 minutes, watching all the time.
    4. Turn the tin as needed.
    5. Take it out as soon as the tips turn golden.
    Heathot grillWait1–2 min, until tips turn golden
    Option B · kitchen blowtorch
    1. Put the pie on a heatproof surface.
    2. Hold the torch about 15 cm from the meringue.
    3. Sweep the flame over the peaks, keeping it moving.
    4. Stop when the tips turn golden.
    Heatblowtorch, flame movingWaituntil tips turn golden
    1–2 minChoose one: A or B
  12. 12

    Chill, then slice

    1. Cool the pie in its tin on a rack for about 45 minutes.
    2. Chill in the fridge for at least 2 hours, so the filling sets. It must be in the fridge within 2 hours of cooking.
    3. Lift the set pie out of the tin.
    4. Slice with a sharp knife.
    5. Wipe the knife between cuts.
    2 hr 45 minWaitcool 45 min on a rackWaitchill 2 hr
